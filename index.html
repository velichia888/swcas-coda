Welcome

You belong here

Saturday Women's Caring & Sharing is a small, friendly women's meeting of Co-Dependents Anonymous. We gather each week to share our experience, strength and hope as we learn to have healthy, loving relationships, starting with ourselves.

Whether this is your first meeting or your five-hundredth, there's a seat saved for you. Come as you are.

Your first meeting

It's normal to feel nervous. Here's what a Saturday morning with us is like.

  1. 1
    Come a few minutes earlyHead to Room #3. Someone will say hello and help you find a seat.
  2. 2
    Listen as long as you likeYou never have to share. Saying "I'll pass" is always fine.
  3. 3
    We read, then we shareA few short CoDA readings open the meeting, then members take turns sharing without crosstalk or advice.
  4. 4
    What's said here stays hereWe use first names only, and we keep each other's stories private.
  5. 5
    There's no costA basket is passed to cover rent and literature. Giving is optional, and newcomers are asked to keep their money.
