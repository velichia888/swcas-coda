What is CoDA?

Co-Dependents Anonymous is a fellowship of people who want healthy and loving relationships. We use the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions, and we support each other by sharing honestly about our own lives. The only requirement for membership is a desire for healthy and loving relationships.

Questions people often ask

Do I have to talk?No. You're welcome to just listen, today and for as long as you like.
Is it religious?CoDA is spiritual, not religious. We meet at a church, but the meeting isn't part of the church. Each of us finds our own understanding of a Higher Power.
Does it cost anything?No dues or fees. We pass a basket to cover rent and literature, and newcomers are encouraged to keep their money for now.
Who can come?This is a women's meeting. Any woman with a desire for healthy and loving relationships is welcome.
Will people know I was there?We use first names only, and what's shared in the room stays in the room.

A few ideas for the week ahead

  • Collect a few phone numbers after the meeting, if you feel comfortable.
  • Read the Twelve Promises and the Twelve Steps on coda.org.
  • The Newcomer's Handbook is a gentle first read.
